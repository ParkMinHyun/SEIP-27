\subsection{RQ2: Contribution of Admission and Pacing}
\label{sec:rq2}

\input{tables/tab_rq2_ablation.tex}

Table~\ref{tab:rq2_ablation} compares admission and pacing separately and together in three cells starting at the static thermal guard's level-4 cutoff.
\emph{No control} enables neither component, the same configuration as Baseline in Table~\ref{tab:rq1_controller_behavior}.
\emph{Admission only} and \emph{Pacing only} enable only the named component, and \emph{Full} enables both.
The Captures column reports the percentage of requested captures completed on time.
The \(M\) and \(S\) columns report the percentages completed on time with the corresponding stage executed, using the same denominator.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Single-component control.}
Only \emph{Full} completed all requested captures on time in all three evaluated cells.
\emph{No control} completed \mbox{24.3--42.3\%} of requested captures, while \emph{Pacing only} completed \mbox{28.1--61.4\%}.
\emph{Admission only} completed all requested captures in the S26 Ultra 12MP cell, but 87.3\% and 90.7\% elsewhere.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Limits of admission alone.}
In the S26 Ultra 12MP cell, \(M\) and \(S\) execution rates under \emph{Admission only} were only 29.3\% and 33.7\%, respectively, compared with 29.0\% for both under \emph{No control}.
In the S26 12MP cell, the corresponding rates were 44.3\% and 51.3\%, but on-time completion was only 90.7\%.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Coordinated control.}
\emph{Full} executed both \(M\) and \(S\) more often than \emph{Admission only} in every cell.
This higher retention came with a responsiveness cost: \emph{Full} activated pacing on 36.7--53.1\% of eligible transitions, with a median positive applied delay of 297--437~ms.
These medians were 37--45\% of the shot-to-shot delay observed when parallel capture was disabled.
