\subsection{RQ2: Contribution of Admission and Pacing}
\label{sec:rq2}

\input{tables/tab_rq2_ablation.tex}

Table~\ref{tab:rq2_ablation} separates the contributions of admission and pacing at starting overheat level 4, where the thermal guard begins suppressing optional stages.
\emph{No control} enables neither component.
\emph{Admission only} and \emph{Pacing only} enable one each, and \emph{Full} enables both.
Captures reports on-time completion, while \(M\) and \(S\) also require the corresponding stage to execute.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Single-component control.}
Only \emph{Full} completed all requested captures on time in every panel.
\emph{No control} completed \mbox{24.3--42.3\%} of requested captures, while \emph{Pacing only} completed \mbox{28.1--61.4\%}.
\emph{Admission only} completed all requested captures in the S26 Ultra 12MP panel, but only 87.3\% and 90.7\% in the other two.
Neither single-component configuration therefore achieved full completion across all three panels.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Limits of admission alone.}
In the S26 Ultra 12MP panel, \emph{Admission only} completed all requested captures on time, but \(M\) and \(S\) executed on only 29.3\% and 33.7\%, respectively.
These rates remained close to the 29.0\% observed for both stages under \emph{No control}, despite the increase in completion.
In the S26 12MP panel, it executed \(M\) and \(S\) more often, on 44.3\% and 51.3\%, but completed only 90.7\% on time.
Admission alone therefore left an unresolved trade-off between completion and stage execution.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Combined control.}
\emph{Full} extends \emph{Admission only} with pacing.
With this addition, \emph{Full} completed every requested capture on time in all three panels while executing both \(M\) and \(S\) more often than \emph{Admission~only} in every panel.
These results are consistent with pacing sharing deadline control with admission, reducing the need to rely on optional-stage skipping alone.
