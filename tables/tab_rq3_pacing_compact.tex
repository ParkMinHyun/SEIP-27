  \begin{minipage}{\columnwidth}
    \tiny\raggedright
    Retrospective pressure is $B+2C-\max(0,T)$, where $B$ is the measured
    backlog, $C$ the realized duration of the admitted Draft sequence, and $T$
    the remaining deadline window; projected overrun denotes nonnegative
    pressure, not an observed Capture Timeout.  The pacing-only envelope is
    $d^{*}_{\mathrm{exec}}=\ceil{[B+2C_{\mathrm{exec}}-\max(0,T)]^{+}/2}$.
    The mandatory floor $d^{*}_{\mathrm{mand}}$ replaces the skippable workload
    in $C_{\mathrm{exec}}$ with DynamicFunction and Encoding, plus measured
    Draft overhead.  A delay in the admission-flexible band deliberately leaves
    optional-work protection to admission instead of making pacing alone cover
    the realized-work envelope.  Demotion means a reduction from Bokeh+Filter
    to Filter-only or Encoding-only execution.  The deployed $2C$ horizon
    represents (i) the Draft that begins after the pacing decision and (ii) the
    Draft of the next capture released by that delay.  Accordingly,
    target-or-next reports whether admission demoted either Draft in the horizon;
    it does not attribute the next admission decision causally to the current
    delay.  The 14 floor misses cluster in four 24MP bursts.  The floor is a
    retrospective sufficient reservation condition, not the timeout boundary.
    All targets were demoted; because $d^{*}_{\mathrm{mand}}$ already excludes
    optional work, this documents coordination rather than explaining away the
    remaining floor deficit.  Target and next captures kept at least 307 and
    276\,ms of realized margin, respectively, and no floor miss produced an
    actual Capture Timeout.  Backlog under-estimation in 14/14 and rising
    headroom in 12/14 during a median 4.78\,s queue residence are observationally
    consistent with queue and thermal drift after the online decision.  Records
    affected by the known timeout-measurement error were removed as invalid
    observations; no valid analyzed run experienced an actual Capture Timeout.
    Watchdog-truncated transitions lack a complete realized Draft duration and
    are excluded only from envelope reconstruction.  Potential delay avoided is
    $d^{*}_{\mathrm{exec}}-d$ within the flexible band; it is not a
    counterfactual end-to-end speedup.  Backlog overlap is
    $\sum\min(d,B)/\sum d$ over paced transitions.  Burst cost is $\sum d$
    divided by the sum of shot-to-shot intervals within the burst.  P50/P95 are
    inclusive percentiles.
