Table~\ref{tab:casestudy_selection} summarizes the selected run relative to its peers.
The run exercises every coordination behavior and is worse than the peer median on all five reported metrics.
We use it to illustrate control interaction rather than typical behavior.

Figure~\ref{fig:casestudy_12mp} traces this 30-capture run in the production-default 12MP mode, without external memory pressure, starting at overheat level 4.
This setting matches the motivating timeout and the level at which the production guard begins suppressing optional stages.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Control interaction.}
The trace shows a repeated two-step response as backlog rises.
Pacing engaged first, and when pressure persisted, admission skipped an optional stage.
Afterward, backlog declined and Slack recovered.
In the first period, pacing began at capture 5 and admission intervened at capture 9, with Slack reaching 27.3\% by capture 15.
The same sequence recurred from capture 21, with admission intervening at capture 23.
Backlog then fell from 63.8\% to 31.8\% of the budget while queue depth remained 6, and Slack recovered to 45.6\% by capture 30.
Together, the trace shows the controller escalating its response as queue pressure persisted.
