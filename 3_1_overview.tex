\subsection{Design Objectives and Execution Contract}
\label{sec:overview}

We introduce the \emph{Budget-Aware Draft Controller} to prevent Capture Timeout caused by inter-capture interference without uniformly suppressing optional Draft image processing.
The controller coordinates two framework-local modules.
\emph{Remaining-Sequence Admission} controls the active Draft Sequence's service demand: using an estimate of its remaining work and live budget, it admits or skips optional image processing.
\emph{Capture-Availability Pacing} controls the timing of application's next capture initiation: using estimated worker occupancy and the remaining deadline window, it postpones delivery of the \texttt{captureAvailable} callback.
Admission may reduce Draft quality, whereas pacing may lengthen the shot-to-shot interval.
Both modules operate within the existing single-worker execution model and require no changes to the HAL or application interface

Figure~\ref{fig:controller-overview} traces one control cycle.
Pacing first decides whether to defer the \texttt{captureAvailable} callback, which determines when the application may issue the next capture.
When a Draft Sequence reaches the worker, the controller refreshes its estimates of queued work and Draft processing time.
During execution, admission compares the estimated remaining work with the live Capture Timeout budget before each optional image-processing stage and decides whether to execute or skip that stage.
After the Draft Sequence completes, the controller updates the online model with the measured duration.
If no Draft remains queued, it also resets the temporary state used to coordinate consecutive captures.

The modules coordinate through their effects on subsequent decisions.
By skipping optional work, admission reduces the workload seen by later pacing decisions, but it cannot recover budget spent waiting for the worker or revise a scheduled delay.
Pacing changes the queue wait and remaining Capture Timeout budget seen by later admission decisions, but it cannot change ongoing work.
