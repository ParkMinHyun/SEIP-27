\subsection{Capture-Availability Pacing}
\label{sec:pacing}

\input{figures/alg_pacing.tex}

\emph{Capture-Availability Pacing} decides whether to deliver \texttt{captureAvailable} immediately or delay it, controlling when the application can accept the next capture.
Algorithm~\ref{alg:pacing} returns the callback delay \(\mathit{delay}_i\) based on projected draft work and the remaining timeout budget, then updates its estimates at draft completion.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Projection.}
For capture \(i\), pacing decides at \(\mathit{now}\), before its draft sequence enters the queue; delivering the callback enables capture \(i+1\).
Pacing maintains \(\hat t_{\mathrm{queue}}\), the predicted completion time of outstanding draft work, and computes the remaining backlog \(\hat B_i\) as the nonnegative difference between this timestamp and \(\mathit{now}\) (line~\ref{alg:pa:backlog}).
The retained deadline \(\mathit{timeout}_{\mathrm{queue}}\) belongs to the newest queued capture; subtracting \(\mathit{now}\) gives its remaining timeout budget \(T_i^{\mathrm{queue}}\) (line~\ref{alg:pa:budget}).
Before any capture is queued, pacing uses a full Capture Timeout window.
Because this backlog excludes the draft sequences of captures \(i\) and \(i+1\), pacing reserves a duration \(\hat C\) for each, yielding the projected workload \(\hat B_i+2\hat C\) (line~\ref{alg:pa:deficit}).

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Decision.}
Pacing delays the callback when the projected workload exceeds \(T_i^{\mathrm{queue}}\).
At line~\ref{alg:pa:delay}, pacing computes the delay in milliseconds as
\begin{equation}
  \mathit{delay}_i=\left\lceil
    \frac{\max\{0,\hat B_i+2\hat C-T_i^{\mathrm{queue}}\}}{2}
  \right\rceil.
  \label{eq:pacing}
\end{equation}
This half-deficit rule is a coordination heuristic that shares the response to deadline pressure with admission while limiting callback delay.
\textsf{ExtendBacklog} then accounts for capture \(i\)'s provisional work using \(\hat P^{\mathrm{eff}}\), the latest point estimate with disabled groups excluded (line~\ref{alg:pa:extend}).
It advances \(\hat t_{\mathrm{queue}}\) from the later of its current value and the delayed callback time by this estimated duration.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Reconciliation.}
At draft completion, pacing receives the configured sequence \(\mathcal K_i\), the measured duration \(C_i\), and admission's updated set of disabled groups \(\mathcal D\).
It excludes stages whose groups are in \(\mathcal D\) to form \(\mathcal K^{\mathrm{eff}}\), then obtains its point estimate \(\hat P^{\mathrm{eff}}\) from the updated shared predictor (lines~\ref{alg:pa:effective}--\ref{alg:pa:point}).
To correct accumulated error in these provisional extensions, \textsf{RebaseBacklog} recomputes the predicted queue completion time from the current completion time using the updated estimate for remaining work (line~\ref{alg:pa:rebuild}).

Pacing also updates \(\hat C\) from measured draft durations to account for variation beyond the point estimate.
\textsf{UpdateDraftDurationHistory} adds \(C_i\) to the duration history \(\mathcal C\), decaying existing weights by \(0.90\) (line~\ref{alg:pa:history}).
\textsf{SelectDraftReserveDuration} returns its weighted \(\tau\)-quantile \(C_\tau\), with \(\tau\) determined from this history by the effective-sample-size rule in Section~\ref{sec:admission} (line~\ref{alg:pa:quantile}); \(C_\tau=0\) when the history is empty.
Pacing sets \(\hat C\) to \(\hat P^{\mathrm{eff}}\) plus any positive excess of \(C_\tau\) over \(\hat P(\mathcal K_i)\) (line~\ref{alg:pa:reserve}).
The history may still include measurements collected before groups were disabled.
Using the full configured sequence as the baseline removes the predicted cost of disabled stages from the historical reserve, while retaining \(\hat P^{\mathrm{eff}}\) as a floor.
