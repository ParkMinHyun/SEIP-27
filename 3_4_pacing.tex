\subsection{Capture-Availability Pacing}
\label{sec:pacing}

\emph{Pacing} maintains a running forecast of draft workload to regulate the next capture opportunity.
It extends this forecast after each decision and rebases it as draft sequences complete.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Delay rule.}
When the \texttt{captureAvailable} callback for capture \(i\) is ready for delivery, \(\mathit{controller}.\textsc{DecidePacingDelay}\) (Algorithm~\ref{alg:overview}, line~\ref{alg:ov:pacing}) computes the next-capture delay as
\begin{equation}
  \mathit{delay}_i
  =
  \left\lceil
    \frac{
      \max\{0,\hat B_i+2\hat R-T_{\mathrm{queue}}\}
    }{2}
  \right\rceil.
  \label{eq:pacing}
\end{equation}

In Equation~\ref{eq:pacing}, \(\hat B_i\) denotes the processing-time backlog predicted by pacing.
Pacing maintains \(\hat t_{\mathrm{drain}}\), the predicted drain time obtained by accumulating point estimates of the draft work in this backlog.
At the pacing decision time,
\begin{equation}
  \hat B_i
  =
  \max\{0,\hat t_{\mathrm{drain}}-\mathit{now}\}.
  \label{eq:backlog}
\end{equation}

The backlog \(\hat B_i\) does not yet include draft work for capture \(i\) or capture \(i+1\), the next capture enabled by this callback.
Pacing therefore reserves a duration \(\hat R\) for each.
Let \(\mathcal K^{\mathrm{adm}}\) denote the configured sequence \(\mathcal K\) after removing optional work disabled by admission.
Pacing computes the sequence reserve from the latest timing state as
\begin{equation}
  \hat R
  =
  \hat P(\mathcal K^{\mathrm{adm}})
  +
  \max\!\left(0,C_\tau-\hat P(\mathcal K)\right),
  \label{eq:reserve}
\end{equation}
where \(C_\tau\) is the recency-weighted \(\tau\)-quantile of completed draft-sequence durations.
The controller selects \(\tau\) from this duration history using the same ESS-based quantile rule as for the residual factors in Section~\ref{sec:admission}.
With all configured stages enabled (\(\mathcal K^{\mathrm{adm}}=\mathcal K\)), pacing uses the larger of \(C_\tau\) and \(\hat P(\mathcal K)\) as its reserve.
After admission disables optional work, pacing adds any positive historical headroom of \(C_\tau\) above \(\hat P(\mathcal K)\) to the point estimate based on \(\mathcal K^{\mathrm{adm}}\).

\(T_{\mathrm{queue}}\) denotes the remaining timeout budget of the most recently enqueued capture.
At the pacing decision point, capture \(i+1\)'s timeout clock has not yet started; \(T_{\mathrm{queue}}\) therefore serves as a queue-level pressure reference rather than capture \(i+1\)'s feasibility deadline.
Thus, \(\hat B_i+2\hat R-T_{\mathrm{queue}}\) measures the amount by which the projected draft work exceeds this queue-level budget.
Finally, pacing delays callback delivery by only half of this excess to limit irreversible shot-to-shot latency, leaving workload reduction to admission.
This heuristic split coordinates pacing and admission.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Backlog extension.}
Pacing provisionally extends \(\hat t_{\mathrm{drain}}\) after each decision so that a subsequent pacing decision accounts for capture \(i\) before its draft sequence enters the queue.
It uses \(\hat P^{\mathrm{last}}\), the latest point estimate of \(\mathcal K^{\mathrm{adm}}\):
\begin{equation}
  \hat t_{\mathrm{drain}}
  \gets
  \max\{\hat t_{\mathrm{drain}},\mathit{now}+\mathit{delay}_i\}
  +\lceil\hat P^{\mathrm{last}}\rceil.
  \label{eq:clock-update}
\end{equation}
The \(\max\) term places the provisional work after the existing drain forecast and the callback release time.
Pacing also increments \(N_{\mathrm{pend}}\), the number of pending captures, after each decision.
A capture is pending from its pacing decision until its draft sequence starts; pacing then decrements the count.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Backlog rebase.}
Pacing rebases the backlog at each draft-sequence completion because provisional extensions use earlier point estimates that can become stale as new durations are observed and as admission disables optional work.
After the shared predictor and admission update their states (Algorithm~\ref{alg:overview}, line~\ref{alg:ov:update}), pacing refreshes the estimate used for future extensions.
Using the current admission state, pacing first sets \(\hat P^{\mathrm{last}}=\hat P(\mathcal K^{\mathrm{adm}})\).
Pacing then rebuilds the drain forecast from the current time as
\begin{equation}
  \hat t_{\mathrm{drain}}
  \gets
  \mathit{now}
  +\left\lceil
    N_{\mathrm{pend}}\cdot\hat P(\mathcal K^{\mathrm{adm}})
  \right\rceil.
  \label{eq:clock-rebase}
\end{equation}
All pending captures share the configured sequence \(\mathcal K\), which cannot change until the draft queue drains, and optional work disabled by admission also remains disabled until then.
Pacing therefore estimates each pending capture with the current \(\mathcal K^{\mathrm{adm}}\).
Draft sequences execute serially, and rebase occurs before the next one starts.
The rebuilt forecast therefore needs no term for an executing draft.
Finally, pacing adds the measured draft-sequence duration \(C_i\) to the history for \(C_\tau\), so the reserve for subsequent captures reflects observed durations.
