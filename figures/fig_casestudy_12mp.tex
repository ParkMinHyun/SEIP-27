  \caption{Case study: 12MP capture without external memory pressure, starting
  at overheat Lv4 (Lv5 from capture~20). Pacing activates at capture~5, four
  captures before admission first drops the multi-frame Bokeh stage~(\(M\)) at
  capture~9; the deadline margin had already fallen to 150~ms one capture
  earlier. Pacing does not fall immediately: admission lowers the cost of new
  captures but cannot erase the queueing delay already accumulated ahead of the
  Draft worker. Once that backlog drains, the lighter \(S\)-only sequence lets
  pacing relax to zero over captures~13--20 (shaded); arrivals accelerate
  again, the backlog rebuilds to 4.5~s, and pacing resumes at capture~21 before
  admission demotes the Filter stage~(\(S\)) at capture~23. All 30 captures
  complete without a Capture Timeout. This session is not the best-performing
  one in its condition (Table~\ref{tab:casestudy_selection}); it is the only
  complete session that exhibits the full admission--pacing coordination
  sequence.}
