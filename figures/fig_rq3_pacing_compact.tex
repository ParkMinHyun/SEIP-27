% Compact coordination-aware RQ3 main-paper figure.  The original RQ3 policy,
% selectivity, and calibration figures remain untouched as historical
% alternatives; docs/rq3-current.md is the authoritative handoff for this pair.
%
% Sources:
%   data/rq3/coordination/envelope_ladder_{12mp_normal,24mp_memory}.csv
% Regenerate with scripts/rq3_coordination_metrics.py.
%
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
% Why this figure and not three panels of rates
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
% An earlier revision had three panels: activation by pressure band, a
% mechanism flow chart, and a stacked bar of the envelope partition.  Every one
% of them was a table in disguise -- eight rates, six boxes of prose, and six
% counts respectively -- and all three now live in
% Table~\ref{tab:rq3_pacing_compact}, where a reader can also cite them.  The
% reference papers in references/ do the same thing: results go in tables, and
% the one figure each paper spends is reserved for something a table cannot
% state.  A figure that restates a table earns nothing but page area.
%
% What a table cannot state here is the SHAPE of the coordination.  The table
% says pacing covered 53 of 79 and 83 of 140 realized-work envelopes; it cannot
% say how large those envelopes were, how far above or below them the applied
% delay landed, or that the 24MP mandatory floor only becomes nonzero in the
% deepest fifth of the order.  That is what this figure carries.
%
% Construction.  One panel per condition.  Every transition whose realized Draft
% work would have required a delay is a column, ordered along x by the size of
% that requirement, so the upper curve is monotone by construction and the x axis
% is a rank rather than a quantity.  For each transition the figure draws:
%
%   curve         the delay the realized Draft work would have required
%   shading       the area under it, i.e. the requirement itself
%   mark          the delay the controller actually applied
%
% The two main categories of Table~\ref{tab:rq3_pacing_compact} are then regions
% rather than mark styles, which is why the marks are all one shape: a mark above
% the curve is a covered envelope and a mark inside the shading is
% admission-flexible.  Labelling the regions instead of the marks also puts the
% reading INSIDE the panel, which is what lets the caption be one line.
%
% The mandatory floor is deliberately NOT drawn.  It is zero on all 79 12MP
% transitions and on 111 of the 140 24MP ones, so joining its values into a curve
% draws a saw-tooth between 0 and 782 ms across the upper half of the 24MP order
% and implies a trend that does not exist.  The floor and its 14 misses are a
% seven-row audit in Table~\ref{tab:rq3_pacing_compact}, which is the right place
% for 14 cases in one condition.  Do not reinstate it as a curve.
%
% Frames.  12MP y to 900 ms, which holds every point (largest applied delay 874).
% 24MP is clipped at 1,600 ms and the panel says so: two applied delays reach
% 2,010 and 2,022 ms, and a frame that held them would spend a third of the panel
% on two marks.  The 24MP curve reaches 1,038 ms, so it occupies the lower half of
% its frame; that is the finding, not a framing accident -- the applied delay
% routinely exceeds what the realized work required.
%
% Claim limits are in tables/tab_rq3_pacing_compact.tex and docs/rq3-current.md.
% In particular the shaded band is a joint-control interpretation, not a claim
% that pacing alone covers the work that eventually ran, and the floor is a
% retrospective sufficient reservation condition rather than the timeout
% boundary.
