  \caption{RQ3 admission--pacing coordination. (a) Online activation against
  pressure reconstructed after the Draft executes; whiskers are 95\%
  burst-cluster bootstrap intervals. (b) The two boundary mismatches.  Pacing
  can precede an admission demotion that later creates retrospective spare.
  Conversely, long queue residence and rising thermal headroom are consistent
  with processing slowdown after a no-pacing decision, producing backlog
  under-estimation; admission provides a partial backstop.  These mechanism
  associations are observational. (c) Decisions with a positive pacing-only
  realized-work envelope $d^{*}_{\mathrm{exec}}$: pacing either covers that
  envelope, stops within the admission-flexible band
  $d^{*}_{\mathrm{mand}}\leq d<d^{*}_{\mathrm{exec}}$, or falls below the
  mandatory-work floor.  The deployed $2C$ horizon comprises the Draft that
  begins after the decision and the next capture's Draft released by the delay.
  Flexible-band annotations therefore report target and target-or-next admission
  demotions; they do not causally attribute the next admission decision to the
  current delay.  The 14 24MP floor misses occurred in four bursts; 11 received
  zero delay, yet all 14 targets were demoted, retained at least 307\,ms realized
  margin, and incurred no actual Capture Timeout.  Because
  $d^{*}_{\mathrm{mand}}$ already excludes optional work, demotion documents
  coordination rather than causally explaining that margin.  All 14 also
  underestimated backlog and 12/14 showed rising headroom during queue residence,
  consistent with retrospective queue/thermal drift.  The floor is a sufficient
  reservation condition, not the timeout boundary.  Records affected by the known
  timeout-measurement error are invalid observations; no valid analyzed run timed
  out.  Segment labels are transition counts.}
