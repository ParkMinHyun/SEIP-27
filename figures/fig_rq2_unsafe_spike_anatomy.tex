  \caption{Anatomy of the eight unsafe fresh-model admits.  Rows M1--M5 are
  Multi-frame decisions and S1--S3 are Single-frame decisions, ordered by
  spike size within each group; each baseline averages the three preceding
  safe admits from the same run and group.  (a)~Remaining-sequence cost,
  normalized by the admission budget of the same decision.  Every group was
  admitted because its predicted upper bound fell below the budget, and every
  one still crossed it, growing $1.22\times$--$2.36\times$ over its
  recent-safe baseline.  (b)~The same increase decomposed through
  \texttt{PostExecutionMetrics}: because a node's wall time equals its CPU
  time divided by \texttt{cpuUtilizationRatio} and the core count, the
  increase splits exactly into the part explained by additional CPU time and
  the part explained by the process obtaining a smaller share of the cores.
  All eight consumed more CPU time ($1.05\times$--$1.53\times$), but the four
  that also lost at least a quarter of their CPU service (M1--M3, S1) are
  exactly the four that grew by more than $1.5\times$; the other four stayed
  within $1.31\times$.  Blocking GC time was zero in every baseline and every
  event, and the overheat level and thermal status were identical to the
  recent-safe baseline in all eight cases, so neither signal could have
  separated these decisions at admission time.}
