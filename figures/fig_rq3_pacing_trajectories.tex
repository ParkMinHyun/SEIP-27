% Data and inclusion protocol are documented in
% tables/tab_rq3_pacing_summary.tex and data/rq3/calibration/summary.csv.

% reported explicitly in Table~\ref{tab:rq3_pacing_summary}.  Marker shape
% exposes, rather than filters away, captures whose admission outcome changed
% the configured workload.

  \caption{RQ3 calibration for decisions with a positive executed-work trace
  target. Each point is one valid decision from a non-timeout 30-shot session;
  the dashed line denotes \(d_i=d_i^{\mathrm{tr}}\). Points below the line are
  insufficient for the executed sequence, whereas points above it are locally
  conservative. Filled circles retain the configured workload; open triangles
  identify decisions whose admission outcome changed it. Zero-target decisions
  are omitted to keep the positive-target calibration legible and are reported
  separately in Table~\ref{tab:rq3_pacing_summary}. Panel (b) uses the actual
  per-capture resolution: Lv0--4 start with two MP24 captures and continue at
  MP12, while Lv5--6 use the production MP12 fallback. The target holds the
  observed trace fixed and is not a global counterfactual optimum.}
  \label{fig:rq3_pacing_calibration}
