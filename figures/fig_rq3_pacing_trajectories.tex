% RQ3: Per-shot policy trajectories and deadline-risk exposure for S26 Ultra.
%
% The figure currently renders the collected No-pacing and Ours series; empty
% policy CSVs add no curve until their factual traces arrive.
%
% Baseline naming follows the implementation
% (patches/rq3-pacing-policy-switch.patch, PolicyType): OURS, THERMAL_LUT,
% QUEUE_EWMA.  The earlier "Static LUT" / "Queue dynamic" labels are retired.
%
% Each policy CSV contains one row per shot and the median, P10, and P90 of
% realized backlog, queue depth, and applied delay.  Those panels plot only
% medians because four uncertainty bands obscured the policy curves.  Panel
% (c) instead plots the median and IQR of cumulative delay: cumulative cost is
% formed within each run before cross-run quantiles are taken, and only the two
% collected policies currently contribute bands.
%
% Panel (d) deliberately complements rather than repeats Table VI.  The table
% reports pooled deadline-risk exposure (No pacing 25.1852%, Ours 6.6667%),
% whereas the figure plots all nine/eight run-level shares from
% risk_exposure_runs.csv.  Horizontal bars mark the run-level medians (26.6667%
% and 3.3333%); p=0.0011 is their two-sided Mann--Whitney comparison.
%
% The S26 Ultra series use the same included runs as
% tables/tab_rq3_pacing_summary.tex: No-pacing from
% 48U_metrics_12MP_normal_0729_AdmitOnly_{1,2}.xlsx (nine runs) and Ours from
% 48U_metrics_12MP_normal_0729.xlsx (eight runs), starting Lv3, 12MP normal.
% The cumulative cost at shot i sums delays on shots 1..i-1, because the delay
% recorded on shot j gates shot j+1.  It therefore starts at zero and its
% shot-30 endpoint equals the full 29-transition run total.  The Ours endpoint
% is 4.6095 s (run-level median); No pacing remains at zero.

  \caption{RQ3 pacing behavior on S26 Ultra under 12MP normal at
  starting thermal level 3.  Panels (a) and (b) show the per-shot median
  backlog and queue depth over nine No-pacing and eight Ours runs.  Panel (c)
  shows how pacing cost accumulates over the burst; its curves and bands are
  the median and IQR of the run-level cumulative delay, respectively, and Ours
  ends at a median of \(4.61\)~s over 29 transitions.
  Panel (d) plots each run's share of shots whose real backlog exceeds 80\% of
  the \(7\,000\)-ms Capture Timeout budget; horizontal bars mark the medians,
  which decrease from 26.7\% to 3.3\% (\(p=.0011\), two-sided Mann--Whitney).
  Table~\ref{tab:rq3_pacing_summary} separately reports the corresponding
  pooled shot-level shares.
  Per-run spread for backlog and queue depth is released with the artifact
  rather than drawn, because four bands per panel obscured the medians.
  Available factual policies are plotted; uncollected policies remain absent.
  The current S26 Ultra No-pacing and Ours curves are provisional because their
  admitted workloads were not matched.}
