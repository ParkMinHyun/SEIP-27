  \caption{Case study (b): 24MP capture mode under external memory pressure,
  starting at overheat Lv3 (Lv4 from capture~25) and never entering the Lv5
  MP12 resolution fallback. As in production, only the first two captures are
  encoded at MP24 (grey band) and the burst continues at MP12. Pacing
  activates at capture~4, two captures before admission drops the multi-frame
  Bokeh stage~(\(M\)) at capture~6. The lighter \(S\)-only sequence lets
  pacing fall to zero for captures~6--8 (shaded); the backlog then rebuilds,
  pacing returns at capture~9 and peaks at 1.3~s, and the deadline margin
  bottoms out at 245~ms before admission demotes the Filter stage~(\(S\)) at
  capture~17. The mandatory-only tail restores the margin to 2.7~s by
  capture~30, and all 30 captures complete without a Capture Timeout. The
  session was selected for mechanism coverage, not performance
  (Table~\ref{tab:casestudy_selection}).}
