\section{Evaluation}

\label{sec:evaluation}

We evaluate whether coordinated admission and pacing enable lightweight multi-frame composition in the draft sequence under parallel capture on the production camera stack.
We assess deadline safety, optional-stage execution, and pacing delay.
We address four research questions:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{RQ\arabic*:}, leftmargin=4.8em]
  \item \textbf{End-to-end effectiveness.}
  Does coordinated control retain optional stages while avoiding Capture Timeout, and at what pacing cost?

  \item \textbf{Contribution of admission and pacing.}
  How do admission and pacing affect the end-to-end outcome, individually and together?

  \item \textbf{Admission decision quality.}
  How accurately does admission distinguish feasible from unsafe draft work, compared with naive approaches?
 
  \item \textbf{Pacing under queue pressure.}
  How do pacing activation and delay relative to backlog vary with queue pressure and capture deadline margins?
\end{enumerate}
