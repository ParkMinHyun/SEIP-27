\subsection{RQ3: Admission Decision Quality}
\label{sec:rq3}

RQ3 reads 8{,}430 enforced admission decisions from 143 full-controller runs
and audits a disjoint always-admit pool of 3{,}746 recorded decisions drawn
from pacing-only runs.
Each decision is labelled retrospectively from the realized trace.
A decision is \emph{feasible} when the measured duration from that stage through Draft completion fits the \(R_{i,j}\) the decision read, and \emph{unsafe} when it exceeds that budget or the watchdog intervenes.
The pair is the realized counterpart of Equation~\ref{eq:admission}, which the runtime tests against \(U\) precisely because the realized duration is not yet available.
The always-admit arm enforces no skip and applies neither the queue-local demotion nor the watchdog of Section~\ref{sec:admission}, so its labels record model misjudgement rather than outcomes an enforced build would produce.
