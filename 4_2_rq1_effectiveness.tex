\subsection{RQ1: End-to-End Effectiveness}
\label{sec:rq1}

RQ1 reports the balanced full-controller set: ten runs in each of the 14
(capture condition, starting level) cells, every one a complete 30-capture session.
It evaluates the end-to-end trade-off through Capture Timeout, optional-stage retention, capture delay, and deadline margin.
A capture counts toward \(M\) or \(S\) only when the corresponding stage has a positive observed duration, and deadline margins are normalized by the Capture Timeout budget.

\input{tables/tab_rq1_end_to_end_summary.tex}

The deployed static guard is the reference these cells should be read against, and its behavior on them follows from its definition rather than from a separate arm.
On the six cells that start at level 4 or above it suppresses optional stages when each Draft Sequence is constructed, so \(M\) and \(S\) stay at zero there for as long as the level holds.
On the remaining cells it permits every optional stage, which is the configuration the Baseline column measures, and that column reaches Capture Timeout at levels 2 and 3 at 12MP and from level 0 onward at 24MP under memory pressure.
The deployed policy therefore withholds the feature where captures are safe and does not prevent the failure where they are not.
