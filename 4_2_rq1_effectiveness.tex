\subsection{RQ1: End-to-End Effectiveness}
\label{sec:rq1}

RQ1 reports the balanced full-controller set: ten runs in each of the 14
(capture condition, starting level) cells, every one a complete 30-capture session.
It evaluates the end-to-end trade-off through Capture Timeout, optional-stage retention, capture delay, and deadline margin.
A capture counts toward \(M\) or \(S\) only when the corresponding stage has a positive observed duration, and deadline margins are normalized by the Capture Timeout budget.
