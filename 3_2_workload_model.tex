\subsection{Runtime State and Estimation Model}
\label{sec:model}

The controller maps each modeled Draft node execution to a \emph{workload}
keyed by stage, a stable output-size bucket, and required variants.  For capture \(i\),
the remaining sequence \(\sigma_{i,j}\) is the ordered workload suffix from node
\(j\) through the Encoding tail; a \emph{whole Draft} is the profiled interval
from profiler initialization through task completion.  A workload has one of
three runtime policies.  \textsc{Optional} workloads---the multi-frame stage
\(M\) and degradable stages in \(S\)---may be skipped and are watchdog-guarded
when admitted.  \textsc{Required} workloads, including Dynamic Function and
frame watermark, always run and are measured but receive no watchdog reserve.
\textsc{Reserved} work also always runs and is protected in that reserve; in the
current pipeline, it is the Encoding interval, which closes when the saved Draft
task completes.  Every remaining workload contributes to the suffix estimate
regardless of policy.  Thus, an upstream Optional decision prices following
Required work, while frame watermark retains its prerequisite decoding when the
rest of the decoration chain is demoted.

Table~\ref{tab:controller-state} summarizes the controller state.  For workload
\(w\), \(\hat p_w=\bar b_w\gamma\), where \(\bar b_w\) is a per-workload
baseline computed with equal sample weights and \(\gamma\) is the
recency-weighted median of observed duration-to-baseline ratios.  The median
dampens an isolated stall while carrying sustained slowdown across workload
families and sizes.

At Draft completion, both quantities are updated from their pre-completion
values, preventing within-capture feedback.
The suffix sum gives \(\hat P(\sigma_{i,j})\); exact-suffix residuals, with a
pooled fallback and monotone tail correction, form the empirical upper estimate
\(U(\sigma_{i,j})\).  The model updates at Draft completion.  A cold size can
reuse a same-family estimate scaled by megapixels, whereas a wholly unseen
family contributes zero until observed.

Two additional quantities price whole-Draft occupancy.  The predictor learns
\(\hat H\) as a recency-weighted mean of residual profiled-Draft time not
covered by workload intervals and adds it once per Draft; during a burst, the
pacer retains \(M_s\), the largest observed profiled Draft interval for size
bucket \(s\).
