\subsection{Workload Model and Online Duration Estimation}
\label{sec:model}
Both control modules require online estimates of remaining draft work.
The controller therefore estimates stage durations from observations of completed draft sequences and combines these estimates for the sequence being considered.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Modeled workload sequence.}
Each workload key \(k\) identifies a processing stage and its configuration.
Capture \(i\)'s draft sequence is represented by the ordered keys \(\mathcal{K}_i=(k_1,\ldots,k_m)\).
At position \(j\), the remaining sequence is \(\mathcal{K}_{i,j}=(k_j,\ldots,k_m)\).
The last key covers mandatory processing from encoding start through draft image saving.
Including this processing lets admission account for the time needed to produce the draft image after the optional stages finish.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Online point estimation.}
The controller maintains a baseline duration \(\hat b(k)\) for each key.
A shared factor \(\gamma\) adjusts these baselines to recent execution conditions.
Multiplying the two gives the predicted duration \(\hat p(k)\), and summing these predictions gives the duration estimate for a modeled sequence \(\mathcal K\):
\begin{equation}
  \hat P(\mathcal{K})
  =\sum_{k\in\mathcal{K}}\hat p(k),
  \qquad
  \hat p(k)=\hat b(k)\,\gamma .
  \label{eq:point-estimate}
\end{equation}
At draft sequence completion, the controller divides each observed duration by the previous \(\gamma\) and incorporates the result into the key's cumulative average \(\hat b(k)\).
When prior baselines are available, the controller updates \(\gamma\) to the median ratio of observed durations to those baselines.
The median limits the influence of isolated stage slowdowns.
An unobserved key has a zero estimate until its first positive duration is recorded at draft sequence completion.
