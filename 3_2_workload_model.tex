\subsection{Workload Model and Online Duration Estimation}
\label{sec:model}
A draft sequence's duration depends on its stages and how fast they execute under current conditions.
The controller therefore learns stage durations from completed draft sequences and combines them according to the sequence being considered.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Modeled workload sequence.}
The controller represents capture \(i\)'s draft sequence as \(\mathcal{K}_i=(k_1,\ldots,k_m)\), where each workload key \(k\) identifies a processing stage and its configuration.
At position \(j\), the remaining sequence is \(\mathcal{K}_{i,j}=(k_j,\ldots,k_m)\).
Its terminal key covers mandatory processing from the start of encoding until the draft image is saved.
Including this terminal interval lets admission account for the time needed to produce the image after the optional stages finish.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Online point estimation.}
For any modeled sequence \(\mathcal K\), the controller sums the predicted per-key durations:
\begin{equation}
  \hat P(\mathcal{K})
  =\sum_{k\in\mathcal{K}}\hat p(k),
  \qquad
  \hat p(k)=\hat b(k)\,\gamma .
  \label{eq:point-estimate}
\end{equation}
The baseline \(\hat b(k)\) represents the stage's long-term processing cost, while the shared factor \(\gamma\) adjusts that cost to recent execution conditions.
At draft sequence completion, the controller updates each baseline with a cumulative average of condition-normalized durations.
It computes \(\gamma\) as the median of measured-to-baseline duration ratios across the stages in the most recently completed sequence.
The median limits the influence of isolated stage spikes, while the shared factor carries broader changes in execution speed into subsequent estimates.

An unobserved workload key has a zero estimate until its first duration is recorded at draft sequence completion.
Section~\ref{sec:admission} explains how admission obtains these initial observations.
Learned histories survive queue drains and are discarded only after the camera application closes and its queued draft sequences finish.
