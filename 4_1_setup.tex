\subsection{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

Table~\ref{tab:setup} summarizes the experimental platform and common run conditions.
The lightweight multi-frame composition targets Portrait mode and requires coordinated HAL and image-solution support; at the time of evaluation, an end-to-end binary carrying both was available only for the Galaxy S26 Ultra.
Unlike other flagship variants, this model has no queue-depth- or feature-specific restrictions on parallel capture beyond the static thermal guard, allowing us to isolate the effects of admission and pacing.
Across configurations, we keep the production-based camera stack and the study recording path fixed and vary only the controller policy.
The static thermal guard is bypassed throughout, so admission is the only mechanism that skips an optional stage.

We evaluate two representative endpoints spanning the operating conditions: the production-default 12MP configuration under normal memory conditions and the more demanding 24MP configuration under memory pressure.
The 24MP condition refers to the requested capture mode; at overheat level~5 and above, the production pipeline falls back to 12MP.
Runs are grouped by the overheat level observed at the first capture; thermal state is otherwise allowed to evolve naturally across the 30 captures.
The ADB loop issues the next capture as soon as capture becomes available, exercising the maximum request rate permitted by the capture-availability path.
