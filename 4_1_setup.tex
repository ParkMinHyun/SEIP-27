\subsection{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

Table~\ref{tab:setup} summarizes the experimental platform and common run conditions.
The lightweight multi-frame composition targets Portrait mode and requires coordinated HAL and image-solution support; at the time of evaluation, an end-to-end PoC binary was available only for the Galaxy S26 Ultra.
Unlike other flagship variants, this model has no queue-depth- or feature-specific restrictions on parallel capture beyond the static thermal guard, allowing us to isolate the effects of admission and pacing.
Across configurations, we keep the production-based camera stack and study instrumentation fixed and vary only the controller policy.

We evaluate two endpoint conditions established by the baseline campaign: 12MP normal capture, the production-default configuration, and 24MP capture under memory pressure, where the uncontrolled pipeline fails earliest.
In 24MP mode, the production capture pipeline falls back to 12MP at Overheat Level~5 and above.
Runs are grouped by the overheat level observed at the first capture, and each run uses a fresh camera application session.
The ADB loop issues the next capture as soon as capture becomes available, exercising the maximum request rate permitted by the capture-availability path.
