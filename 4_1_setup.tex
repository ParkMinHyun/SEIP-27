\subsection{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

Table~\ref{tab:setup} summarizes the experimental platform and common run conditions.
The evaluated lightweight multi-frame composition targets Portrait mode and requires coordinated HAL and image-solution support; during the study, an end-to-end PoC binary was available only for the Galaxy S26 Ultra.
This model also lacks the additional queue-depth- and feature-specific parallel-capture guards used by other flagship variants, allowing admission and pacing to be evaluated without interference from those guards.
Across configurations, the same production-based camera stack and study instrumentation are used; only the controller policy differs.

We evaluate two endpoint conditions established by the baseline campaign: 12MP normal capture, the production-default configuration, and 24MP capture under memory pressure, where the uncontrolled pipeline fails earliest.
In 24MP mode, the production pipeline falls back to 12MP at Overheat Level~5 and above.
Runs are grouped by the overheat level observed at the first capture, and each run starts from a fresh camera application session.
The ADB loop issues the next capture immediately when capture becomes available, creating a worst-case continuous-capture workload more aggressive than typical manual operation.
