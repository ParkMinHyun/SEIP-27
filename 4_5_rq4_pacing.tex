\subsection{RQ4: Pacing-Delay Sizing}
\label{sec:rq4}

RQ4 analyzes 1{,}920 and 1{,}861 pacing decisions over 70 and 69 full-controller runs.
For each decision, the study recording path retains the decision-time backlog and budget estimates used by Equation~\ref{eq:pacing}, the requested delay \(d_i\), and the realized timing used for retrospective sizing.
A 30-capture run yields 29 capture-availability transitions; pacing activation rates and \(d_i\) percentiles are therefore computed over those transitions, excluding the delay associated with the final capture because it gates no subsequent capture.
Deadline margins are normalized by the Capture Timeout budget.
