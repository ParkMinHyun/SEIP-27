% Activation rises from 0.0\% of the decisions taken while the backlog stayed under a tenth of the budget to 83.4\% of those taken while it held at least half, and the ordering holds on each device read alone.
% The size of the delay moves the other way: its median is 29.1\% of the backlog on the lightest band that paced at all and falls to 8.3\% on the heaviest, and all but 0.4\% of the 453~s of delay applied over the condition elapsed before that backlog had drained.
% Pacing therefore trims the rate at which captures arrive rather than trying to drain what is already queued, and it never idles the worker to do so.
